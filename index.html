Abstract

Temporal imaging of plants in controlled environments helps scientists better understand growth and biological processes. However, analyzing large volumes of images has been limited by a lack of accessible, standardized datasets and automated analytical tools. This website presents a comprehensive dataset and analysis pipeline for plant phenotyping. The system integrates image acquisition, segmentation, feature extraction, and interpretation to support high-throughput phenotyping in greenhouse settings. The dataset includes multiple plant species and developmental stages, enabling researchers to benchmark detection and trait estimation approaches at scale.

Plant Growth and Phenotyping Version 2 Dataset

This study introduces the expanded Plant Growth and Phenotyping Version 2 dataset (PGP v2), which substantially increases both scale and diversity from the initial release, comprising approximately 220,000 plant images spanning four crop species—corn, cotton, rice, and sorghum—under controlled greenhouse conditions. Across the dataset, each plant image is paired with complementary metadata describing treatment variables, environmental conditions, and temporal indices, creating a standardized platform for training and benchmarking analytical models. The dataset was designed to support reproducible monitoring of phenological development, stress responses, and trait estimation across multiple plant growth stages while preserving the fidelity required for downstream segmentation and classification workflows.
